Optional USC-restricted planners
--------------------------------

The following optional solver implementations are licensed under University
of Southern California research licenses rather than an OSI-approved
open-source license. Their texts permit use, copying, modification, and
distribution for educational, research, and non-profit purposes subject to the
stated conditions. Commercial use requires separate permission from USC.

The standard setup does not fetch or build these implementations. Their pinned
submodule references use update=none; the separate targeted --checkout command
in README.md obtains them directly from their source repositories. Bundled
builds additionally require GRACE_ENABLE_USC_PLANNERS=ON (for example via the
usc-grid preset). GRACE-owned adapters and registry metadata remain available
without including the solver implementations.

This opt-in mechanism is not a license exception or a determination of
compatibility with GRACE's AGPL-3.0-only license. Source plugins link solver code;
obtain appropriate legal review or permissions before redistributing combined
artifacts or using the solvers outside their permitted purposes. Existing local
checkouts and build outputs are not removed when opting out; they must not be
included accidentally in standard distributions.
